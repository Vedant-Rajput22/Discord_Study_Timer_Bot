Discord Bot Quick Guide

How voice tracking works and available slash commands

🎙️ How Study Tracking Works

Whenever you join any voice channel on the server, the bot starts recording your study session automatically. When you leave, it calculates your session length, updates your daily total, and maintains your daily streak!

💡 Pro-Tip: The Discord AFK channel is automatically excluded so away-time is never counted as study time.

⚡ Available Slash Commands

/stats [user] View personal study time today, this week, month, streak, and rank.
/leaderboard [timeframe] View top study champions for Today, This Week, or All-Time.
/active See who is actively studying in voice channels right now.
/dashboard Direct link to open this live analytics website in your browser.
